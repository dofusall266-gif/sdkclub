**In short**: Sudoku Club ([sudoku-club.com](/)) is a 100% free sudoku site, playable immediately without creating an account, with 4 difficulty levels, a ranked daily challenge, verified single-solution grids, and free PDF printing. This article explains why we sincerely think it's the best option if you just want to play, without friction — and who is behind the site.

## What Sudoku Club does better than elsewhere

### Zero friction to start playing

No account to create, no email to confirm, no login screen before you place your first digit. You arrive on the site, pick your level, and play. Your stats (best times, streak of consecutive days) stay saved on your own device, with no need for us to identify you.

### Truly verified grids

Not all grids are equal. A poorly designed sudoku generator can produce grids with several solutions, or grids that secretly force you to guess rather than deduce. Every Sudoku Club grid is verified algorithmically before being offered: a single possible solution, reachable by pure logic, at every difficulty level.

### A real daily challenge, with a ranking

The [daily challenge](/defi) offers the same grid to all players, every day, with a public ranking based on time and number of mistakes. It's what turns a simple grid into a small shared ritual — a bit like daily games such as Wordle, but for logic lovers.

### Four levels, to really progress

Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert: enough to start without getting discouraged and progress up to advanced techniques (hidden pairs, X-Wing, Swordfish...), all explained step by step on our [techniques page](/techniques).

### Printable, for free

Want a grid on paper rather than on screen? The print feature generates a clean PDF, with or without the solution, in one click — useful for playing offline or preparing a grid for someone else.

## Who are we?

Sudoku Club was born from a small team's passion for this logic game — the wish to offer, plainly, the site we would have liked to find when simply looking for a well-designed sudoku grid: no account to create, no dubious grids, no paywall to unlock a difficulty level.

That passion extends to video as well, on our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@StudyMd667), where we share grid solves and explanations of solving techniques for those who prefer to learn by listening, with on-screen examples, rather than by reading an article.

A question, a buggy grid to report, a suggestion? Our [contact page](/contact) is open to all messages — we read them all.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Sudoku Club really free?**
Yes, entirely — no difficulty level and no feature is reserved for a paid subscription.

**Do I need to create an account to play?**
No. You can play right away. A local identifier is simply generated on your device to remember your stats (best times, current streak) and let you take part in the daily challenge ranking under the nickname of your choice.

**What devices can I play on?**
Sudoku Club works in any browser, on a computer, tablet or mobile, with nothing to install.

**Can I print a grid to play on paper?**
Yes, the print feature is free and generates a clean PDF of the chosen grid, with or without the solution.
